# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Open-source maintainers, agent-framework users, and evaluators who need to understand what SACP adds to long-running AI work.

They are usually reading docs, reviewing receipts, or comparing agent outputs across handoffs.

## Product Purpose

SACP turns messy AI work into auditable work records. The demo page should make the protocol understandable in under two minutes by showing how receipts, evidence, next owner, and human decision boundaries change the result.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, trustworthy, slightly ceremonial.

## Anti-references

Generic AI landing pages, purple gradient hero sections, noisy dashboard cosplay, vague hype copy, and any design that suggests SACP guarantees correctness.

## Design Principles

- Show the audit layer, not just the claim.
- Contrast raw chat with structured receipt state.
- Make evidence, next owner, and human approval visible.
- Keep the protocol small and legible.
- Use concrete examples instead of abstract promises.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, keyboard navigation, readable type on mobile, and reduced-motion support.
